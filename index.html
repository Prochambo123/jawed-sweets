Our Story

Spreading Sweetness Since 1985

Founded in the heart of Sillanwali, Sargodha, Jawed Sweets has been a trusted name for over three decades. What started as a small family shop has grown into a beloved destination for authentic Pakistani mithai.

Every sweet we make follows traditional recipes passed down through generations. We use only pure desi ghee, premium dry fruits, and the finest ingredients — because quality is not just a promise, it's our legacy.
